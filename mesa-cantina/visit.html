Over the bridge, up the beach

½ MInorth of the San Pedro bridge: half a mile

Town sits south of the Boca del Rio. Cross the bridge, head north along the beach side, and we are on the sand facing the reef.

  • Route from town

Getting here

  • By golf cart

    1. From town, drive north to the bridge over the Boca del Rio.
    2. Cross the bridge and keep heading north, sea on your right.
    3. About half a mile on, turn toward the beach. Exact turn and landmark to confirm

    Most carts do it in a few easy minutes from the bridge. Confirm drive time

  • By water taxi or boat

    Staying up the caye? Ask your water taxi or hotel boat for Mesa, just north of the bridge on the beach side. Confirm whether Mesa has its own dock, and the nearest water taxi stop

    Coming by private boat? WhatsApp us before you set off and we will tell you where to tie up. Mooring details to confirm

  • On foot or by bike

    From the bridge it is a flat half mile north, about ten minutes on foot or a few by bike. Walk the beach and you arrive with sand on your feet, which is the right way to arrive. Confirm the beach is walkable the whole way

    After dark, bring a light or take a cart back over the bridge.

Parking. Golf cart parking details to confirm: where, how many carts, and whether it is free for guests
