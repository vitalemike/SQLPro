Strong coffee at eight. Cold rum at sundown.

The Cantina is the bar side of Mesa. The espresso machine wakes the beach up, the rail sees the sun off, and the table on the sand stays the same all day. Only the light changes.

House cocktails

Built around what the island does best: aged Belizean rum, fresh lime, coconut, and hibiscus steeped red. The habanero is from down the road. Ask for it gentle or ask for it properly.

  • Jamaica Sundown

    BZ$20

    Mezcal, hibiscus steeped as agua de jamaica, lime, a pinch of salt, topped with soda. Red as the last ten minutes of light.

  • Last Water Taxi

    BZ$18

    Aged Belizean rum, lime, raw cane sugar, bitters. Short, stiff, and worth missing the boat for.

  • Bridge Run

    BZ$18

    White island rum, coconut cream, fresh pineapple, grated nutmeg. Shaken cold, served colder.

  • North of the Bridge

    BZ$20

    Mezcal, passion fruit, lime, agave, three drops of habanero tincture. Smoke first, heat second.

  • Habanero Paloma

    BZ$18

    Blanco tequila, fresh grapefruit, lime, soda, habanero-salt rim.

  • Reef Night

    BZ$19

    Spiced Belizean rum, cold brew from the coffee bar, coconut milk. The espresso machine works a double shift.

  • Golf Cart Lemonade

    BZ$16

    White rum, fresh-squeezed lemonade, a float of hibiscus. Tall, easy, made for the ride home with someone else driving.

  • Sundown Spritz Zero proof

    BZ$10

    Jamaica, lime, fresh ginger, soda. All the colour, none of the rum.
