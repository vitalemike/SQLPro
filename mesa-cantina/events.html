Nights on the sand, parties by the water

Something happens at Mesa most nights of the week, and any night can be yours. Pull the tables onto the beach, light the lanterns, and let the kitchen feed your people while the sun drops behind the lagoon.

What's included

Set menus

Built with the chef around your group and the season. Every menu carries vegetarian dishes as standard, not as an afterthought.

  • Morning: brunch spread with coconut French toast and fresh fruit
  • Evening: shared starters, family-style mains, dessert
  • Dietary needs handled ahead, not on the night
Open bar packages

Priced per guest, by the hour. Pick a level and we keep the glasses full.

  • Beer and wine
  • House cocktails, rum punch on arrival
  • Premium spirits and a signature drink named for your event
Beach setup

Tables carried down to the sand, linens, lanterns and a clear path for anyone arriving by water taxi.

  • Long tables or scattered lounge seating
  • String lights and lanterns for after sundown
  • Space for a guitarist or a playlist

Package pricing in BZ$ to be supplied by the owner. US$ accepted at 2:1.
