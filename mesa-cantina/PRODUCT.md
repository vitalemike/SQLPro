# Mesa Cantina San Pedro — Product

## What it is
Beachfront restaurant and cantina on Ambergris Caye, Belize. It started as Mesa Cafe (Vilma Linda Plaza, Tarpon St, breakfast and lunch), became Mesa Bistro, and moved to the beach about 0.5 mi north of the San Pedro bridge as Mesa Cantina San Pedro. It serves breakfast all morning, lunch and dinner, with a bar side at night.

1. Tourists staying north of the bridge (golf cart or water taxi), browsing on a phone, deciding where to eat today. Jobs: what's good, when are you open, how do I get there, can I book.
